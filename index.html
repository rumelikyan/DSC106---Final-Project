Understanding Physiological Stress Responses

The study from which this data was extracted tracked students' nervous systems across three exams: midterm 1, midterm 2, and the final exam. In this paper, we want to determine if there is a predictive relationship between stress from the nervous system and exam performance.

Project Question: How do students' stress levels fluctuate across Exams?
Dataset: A Wearable Exam Stress Dataset for Predicting Cognitive Performance in Real-World Settings
Our project develops dynamic, interactive visualizations of physiological data—including HR (heart rate), EDA (data from the electrodermal activity sensor), TEMP, and BVP (data from a photoplethysmograph)—collected during exam sessions.

Scroll down to explore how different physiological signals change during exams.
